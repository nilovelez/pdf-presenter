PDF Diva

A presenter view for any PDF.

Slides fullscreen on the projector, presenter view on your screen:
current slide, next slide, timer and page count.
Free and open source, for Windows, Mac and Linux.

Windows installer macOS Linux (.deb)

Windows 10 and 11 · macOS 13 or later · Debian and Ubuntu · Version 1.4.0 · GPL-3.0-or-later

Built for the booth

The app you keep installed for when a speaker turns up with a PDF on a USB stick. It does one thing, and it does it on cue.

Presenter view

Current slide, next slide, timer and “Page 3 of 30” on your screen. The audience only sees the slide.

Works with any clicker

Page Up, Page Down and the arrow keys, so every standard presentation remote just works.

Finds the projector

Fullscreen on the projector, and you choose what each display shows, even mid-show. Plug and unplug without losing the show.

Black screen on cue

Press B to black out the screen, press it again to bring the slide back.

Nothing else to install

The PDF renderer comes built in. No Acrobat, no Office, no surprises on someone else’s laptop.

Free and open source

No account, no ads, no telemetry. The code is on GitHub.

Know your cues

The whole show runs from the keyboard or a clicker. No menus in front of the audience.
